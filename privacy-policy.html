Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Flirta ("Flirta," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Flirta iOS app and related services. Flirta is a private shared space for two linked partners.

Short version: We use your information to provide your account and shared couple space. We do not sell your personal information. You control optional permissions such as Photos, Location, Notifications, and App Tracking Transparency.

1. Information We Collect

Account and profile information

When you create or access an account, we may collect your email address, account identifier, name, birthday, gender, authentication details, and the sign-in method you choose, such as email or Sign in with Apple. If Apple provides a private relay address, we use that address for the account.

Shared content

When you use Flirta, you may create or receive daily answers, date ideas and plans, places, memories, captions, reactions, notes, milestones, badges, invite and pairing information, and reports. Photos you choose for an answer, memory, or date recap are uploaded when needed to sync that shared content with your linked partner. Content in a shared space is visible to the two linked partners, subject to the controls described in the app.

Location information

When you grant permission while using a location feature, Flirta may use your device location to calculate an approximate distance and to find nearby date spots. We do not show your exact live location to your partner. Some date or memory records may include the place or coordinates you choose to save.

Device, usage, and diagnostic information

We receive information such as device model, iOS version, app version, language, time zone, locale, a device or installation identifier, IP address, and request timestamps. We also receive app interaction and performance events used to understand reliability and improve Flirta.

Notifications and attribution

If you allow notifications, Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging provide a device push token so we can deliver daily questions, reminders, and partner activity notifications. Adjust may process advertising and install attribution information. Flirta requests App Tracking Transparency permission before using data that requires that permission for attribution. You can decline or change this permission in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide sign-in, profile, pairing, shared answers, dates, memories, reminders, and safety features.
  • Sync content between the two linked partners and show the controls each person selects.
  • Calculate distance and provide nearby place results when you request those features.
  • Send notifications you enable and maintain notification delivery.
  • Measure app performance, diagnose errors, understand feature use, and improve the service.
  • Protect the service, investigate reports, prevent abuse, and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to valid legal requests.

3. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to operate Flirta:

  • Your linked partner: Shared answers, dates, memories, and other content you choose to put in your couple space.
  • Service providers: Our account and journal infrastructure providers, image storage providers, Apple services, Firebase Cloud Messaging, and Adjust process information on our behalf.
  • Safety and legal purposes: We may disclose information to investigate abuse, protect users, comply with law, or protect our rights.
  • Business changes: Information may transfer as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law.

Flirta does not include a public feed, public profile search, or chat with strangers.

4. Storage and Security

Some settings and cached data are stored on your device. Account and shared-space data are processed on our servers, and selected shared photos are stored through our media infrastructure. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information in transit and at rest. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

5. Retention and Account Deletion

We keep information while it is needed to provide the service, meet legal requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. You can request account deletion in the app under Us > Account > Delete account. We then delete or de-identify the account and associated Flirta data we control, subject to limited retention required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or backups that expire in the ordinary course. Your partner may retain copies of content they independently exported or saved.

6. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can manage iOS permissions in Settings, use the in-app account controls, or contact us at support@flirta.app. We may need to verify your request before completing it.

7. Children

Flirta is intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it where required.

8. International Processing

Your information may be processed in countries other than the one where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy when Flirta changes or legal requirements change. We will post the updated version here and change the "Last updated" date. Your continued use of Flirta after an update means the updated Policy applies to your use of the service.
