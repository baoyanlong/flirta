Contact Us

Email support@flirta.app. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, a description of the issue, and screenshots when helpful. We aim to reply within 1–3 business days.

Common Questions

I cannot sign in.

Check your connection and confirm that you are using the same email or Apple sign-in method used to create your account. If the problem continues, email support from the address associated with your account.

How do I connect my partner?

Open the invite flow, share your invite code, and have your partner enter it from their Flirta account. A shared space is created after the connection is accepted.

How do I manage permissions?

Flirta requests Photos, Location, Notifications, and App Tracking Transparency only when the related feature needs them. You can change any choice later in iOS Settings > Flirta.

How do I report or block someone?

Use the menu on the relevant answer, note, memory reply, or partner page to report or block. You can also hide individual content from your own view. Reports help us investigate violations of our Terms of Service.

How do I delete my account?

Open Us > Account > Delete account and confirm. This removes your profile and Flirta data we control, subject to the limited retention described in our Privacy Policy.
